Pocket Sort Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

Information stored on the device

Pocket Sort Grid stores game progress, settings, consent status, and the cached Remove Ads entitlement locally on the player's device. Locally recorded gameplay analytics are not uploaded to Saygili Games by the current version.

Advertising, consent, and purchases

The production version uses Google Mobile Ads for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertising; Google User Messaging Platform for privacy choices; and Google Play Billing for the optional Remove Ads purchase. These Google services may process device identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, diagnostics, approximate location derived from network information, and purchase information according to Google's policies and the choices available to the player.

Players can reopen advertising privacy choices from the in-game Settings screen. Purchasing Remove Ads disables banner and interstitial placements. Rewarded ads remain optional and open only after a player requests the associated reward.

Children and sensitive permissions

The game is not directed to children under 13. Saygili Games does not knowingly request a player's name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data through the game.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is retained until the app data is cleared or the app is removed. The game disables Android cloud backup, so uninstalling the app removes this local data. Google may retain advertising and purchase information under its own policies.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact bhdrsaygili@gmail.com.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
